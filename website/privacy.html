Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy describes how the Smart Office Android application (“Smart Office”, “we”) handles information in the app and its optional Bike Tracker Google Drive backup.

Information handled

Depending on the features used, the app may handle motorcycle profiles, ride history, GPS route points, distance, speed, fuel records, maintenance records and settings. These are used to provide tracking, history and reporting features.

Location

Bike Tracker may access device location while tracking, including background location when the required Android permission is granted and an active tracking service is running. Location points are used to record and display ride routes. You can control permissions in Android settings and stop tracking in the app.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect a Google Account, the app uses Google sign-in and requests Drive permission to create or update the Smart Office backup file in your own Drive. Your account email may be used to identify the connected account and show connection status. This feature is optional and requires your consent. The intended Drive use is the app's backup file, not unrelated Drive files. You can disconnect in the app and revoke access in your Google Account security settings.

Storage and sharing

App records are stored locally on your device. If you enable Drive backup, selected backup data is transmitted to and stored in your Google Drive under Google's terms. We do not operate a separate Smart Office cloud database for this backup feature. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

Google Sign-In and Google Drive API are provided by Google and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy and terms. Route maps may use OpenStreetMap data and services, whose terms and attribution apply when map tiles are loaded. These services may receive technical connection information.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Backup files remain in Google Drive until you delete them there. Disconnecting stops future app access but does not automatically delete an existing backup. Keep independent copies of important data.

Security

We use platform permissions and Google authorization to limit access. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and Google Account.
